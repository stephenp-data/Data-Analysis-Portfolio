# Customer Churn Rate Case Study

## BUSINESS PROBLEM
Understand why customers churn and identify strategies to improve retention.

## DATASET
Telco Customer Churn Dataset  
Source: IBM Sample Data / Kaggle

## KPIs
- Overall churn rate
- Churn by contract type
- Customer lifetime indicators

## KEY FINDINGS
- Month-to-month customers churn significantly more than long-term contracts
- Longer tenure correlates strongly with retention

## BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
- Incentivize customers to switch to annual contracts
- Target high-risk segments with retention offers
