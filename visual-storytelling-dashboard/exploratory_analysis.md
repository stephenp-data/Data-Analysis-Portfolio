# EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS SUMMARY

## KEY OBSERVATIONS

### 1. REVENUE CONCENTRATION
Revenue is heavily concentrated in the top three regions, indicating
a reliance on a limited number of markets for overall performance.

### 2. CATEGORY PROFITABILITY
The Furniture category shows declining profitability despite stable sales,
suggesting margin pressure or rising costs.

### 3. SHIPPING COST IMPACT
High shipping costs are contributing to margin loss, particularly in
lower-margin product categories.

## BUSINESS IMPLICATIONS
- Over-reliance on a small number of regions increases risk
- Cost optimization is needed in underperforming categories
- Shipping strategy should be reviewed to protect margins
