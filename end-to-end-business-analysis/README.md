# End-to-End Business Analysis

## OBJECTIVE
Analyze company revenue data to identify trends, key performance indicators,
and actionable business insights.

## DATASET
Online Retail II Dataset  
Source: UCI Machine Learning Repository / Kaggle

Due to file size, raw data is not stored in this repository.

## METHODOLOGY
1. Data cleaning and preprocessing
2. KPI calculation (revenue, growth rates)
3. Trend analysis
4. Visualization
5. Business recommendations

## KEY INSIGHTS
- Revenue shows strong seasonality with Q4 peaks
- A small percentage of products drive the majority of revenue

## RECOMMENDATIONS
- Increase inventory and marketing spend ahead of peak seasons
- Focus retention efforts on high-value customers
